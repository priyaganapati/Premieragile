The book AGILE51 SUCCESS FACTORS is designed to help you succeed in product development, project management, and personal or organizational transformation. It offers practical guidance for implementing Agile and Scrum within teams and organizations.

About The Author

Suresh Konduru is a globally recognized Certified Scrum Trainer (CST) with more than 26 years of experience in Agile transformation, coaching, leadership development, and product delivery management.

He has trained over 20,000 professionals across 35 countries, receiving top ratings for his workshops. A speaker at numerous global conferences, Suresh is known for his interactive, no-PowerPoint sessions that combine real-world examples with Brain Science learning techniques.

He is the co-creator of the Expedia Coaching Model and the founder of the lifelong mentoring program, 'Ask Suresh Konduru' (ASK). Suresh’s impact on the Agile community continues through his active participation and leadership in events like the Regional Scrum Gathering conferences in Hyderabad.
